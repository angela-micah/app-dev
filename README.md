## The Chronicles of Narnia
**Narnia** is a fantasy series about children who enter a magical world through a wardrobe.

### Main Characters
- Peter, Susan, Edmund, and Lucy
- Aslan the Lion

## The Maze Runner
**The Maze Runner** is a sci-fi series about teens who must escape a deadly maze.

### Main Characters
