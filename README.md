# My Favorite Series and Movies

Hi! This is my **first repository**, and here are my *favorite* series/movies.

---

## 1. Harry Potter
**Harry Potter** is a fantasy series about a young wizard who studies at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

- Main characters: Harry, Hermione, Ron
- Main villain: Lord Voldemort
- Why I like it: magic, friendship, and adventure

> "It does not do to dwell on dreams and forget to live."

## 2. The Chronicles of Narnia
**Narnia** follows children who enter a magical world through a wardrobe.

- Main characters: Peter, Susan, Edmund, Lucy
- Famous character: *Aslan the Lion*
- Why I like it: talking animals and a good-versus-evil story

## 3. The Maze Runner
**The Maze Runner** is a dystopian sci-fi series where teens must escape a deadly maze.

- Main characters: Thomas, Newt, Minho
- Why I like it: action, mystery, and teamwork

---

## Top 3 Favorites in Order
1. Harry Potter
2. Narnia
3. Maze Runner
